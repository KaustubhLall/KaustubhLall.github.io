Support between appointments

Therapy questions can come up outside clinic hours: how to use equipment, understand a score, or troubleshoot a concern. Dawn is ResMed's generative-AI sleep-health assistant for questions about its products, CPAP therapy, and equipment, available through the myAir app and on the web. Public materials describe it as experimental, warn that it can make mistakes, and say it does not replace qualified care.

My role in the released system

I built Dawn from early architecture through production launch as one of its core machine-learning engineers. One shared backend powers both the myAir app and web experiences, and I built both surfaces.

Public record and scope

At the September 15, 2026 Morgan Stanley Global Healthcare Conference, ResMed CEO Mick Farrell said Dawn had received 1.5 million inquiries to date and described those interactions as people on therapy engaging. In the October 24, 2024 Q1 FY25 call, he said that after a few months in the US market, 25% of visitors initiated a Dawn session and that direct-to-live-human contact-center query volume had fallen 40%. These are company-reported product outcomes, not measures of my individual impact. The Q1 passage does not define the visitor denominator or comparison window.
